About

I am an Associate Professor of Econometrics at Karamanoğlu Mehmetbey University, Department of Data Science and Analytics. My research applies rigorous quantitative methods to questions in economic growth, institutions, energy and the environment, public finance, and Islamic finance.

My methodological work centres on panel data econometrics, time-series analysis, and applied macroeconometrics — including cross-sectionally dependent panels, distributional and causal estimators, and Bayesian volatility modelling. I also teach undergraduate courses in probability, statistics, and research methods, and serve as a peer reviewer and methodology consultant.
